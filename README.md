1. Overview of the Case Study
The Bright Coffee Shop case study analysed sales performance across three stores from 1 January to 30 June 2023. The objective was to understand revenue performance, customer purchasing patterns, product performance and sales trends.

The analysis aimed to identify opportunities to increase revenue, improve product sales, optimise staffing and support data-driven business decisions.
2. Process: How the Case Study Was Done
1. Data preparation: Used the sales dataset covering January to June 2023.
2. Data analysis: Examined revenue, transactions, quantities sold, average transaction value and unit prices.
3. Product analysis: Identified top-performing and lowest-performing products and categories.
4. Trend analysis: Compared monthly, daily and time-of-day sales performance.
5. Store analysis: Compared revenue across the three stores.
6. Visualisation: Developed an interactive dashboard with filters and charts to explore sales performance.
7. Interpretation: Identified business insights and developed recommendations for the CEO.

Key business insights
- Revenue increased by 98.2% between January and June.
- Average month-on-month revenue growth was 14.8%.
- Morning sales accounted for 58.7% of revenue.
- Night sales represented only 1.4%, indicating an opportunity to explore targeted promotions.
- Coffee dominated sales, contributing 46.1% of revenue.
- Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue among the three stores.
- Cappuccino Lg was the highest revenue-generating product.
4. CEO Recommendations
1. Increase stock availability of top-selling products.
2. Introduce targeted promotions during quieter night hours.
3. Allocate more staff during peak morning hours.
4. Introduce product bundles to increase average transaction value.
5. Investigate the success of Hell's Kitchen and apply relevant practices across other stores.
6. Introduce customer loyalty initiatives to encourage repeat purchases and increase the average transaction value.
